# Metrics

## Detection
- Precision
- Recall
- F1 score

## Business
- Total revenue at risk
- Recovery attempts
- Successful recoveries
- Revenue recovered
- Recovery rate

## Safety / Operations
- False-positive cost
- Unnecessary intervention rate
- Escalation rate
- Guardrail blocks
- Average recovery latency

Never report only cherry-picked examples. Evaluate on a held-out batch.
